# Shrines Of Creation

Shrines of Creation are rumored places where Blackmar can rewrite a Mythic soul. They are not ordinary prayer shrines. They are older, stranger, and far less generous.

Only the choices a shrine presents in game are available. A rumor, ruined altar, or unresponsive shrine may be lore rather than an open path.

## Finding A Shrine

Shrines are hidden behind exploration and character history. Leads may come from Mythic quests, dreams, omens, pilgrimages, rare books, domain relics, or people who speak differently to a soul they recognize.

The shrine itself should make its demand clear once a character is able to understand it. `examine` the room and its features, read inscriptions, and pay attention to unusual responses to carried relics or completed stories.

## What A Shrine May Ask

A shrine can demand more than the level reset of a normal remort. Possible offerings include a regional relic, difficult craftwork, a completed pilgrimage, service to a place or spirit, a preserved memory, or a burden that follows the character into the next life.

Do not assume an offering is safe or refundable. Review every choice the shrine presents before confirming a rite.

## The Shape Of A Rite

A shrine encounter generally follows the same rhythm:

1. Discover the shrine and learn why it has answered.
2. Read or sense its demand.
3. Complete its trial or bring the requested offering.
4. Review the boon, burden, class path, clade, or memory it offers.
5. Confirm the rite only when its consequences are understood.

Some shrines may remain dormant for a character who lacks the right history. Others may accept an offering, impose a delay, awaken guardians, or leave a temporary mark after a failed attempt. Failure should not delete the character, but it may still cost time or valuables.

## Creation Marks And Discovery

A completed rite may leave a mark that survives rebirth. Such marks can identify a class or clade path, preserve a narrow memory, reveal an occult sigil, record a debt, or help another shrine recognize what the soul has already endured.

These marks are character history, not ordinary carried items. Use the commands or shrine text presented during the rite to understand them.

## Etiquette

Treat a Shrine of Creation as both a story place and a permanent character decision. Read before offering, do not interrupt another player's rite, and do not rely on a rumor when the shrine itself says otherwise.
