# The Bodachian Fallows

The continental mistlands, Gloamspire, and the outlying Fallows regions are
dangerous destinations for levels 200-250. Aerial and magical arrivals begin
at the crown of Gloamspire; Fallows Cross remains the village wayshard refuge.

The Bodachian Fallows are a continent where the world is slowly forgetting
itself. Fields lie under permanent dusk. Roads remain after their destinations
vanish from signs and maps. Old houses keep lights in windows no one admits to
lighting. Graves are too shallow, bells lose notes, and fog closes around
places whose names survive only in somebody's pocket ledger.

The Fallows make fear local. They are not a distant underworld or a fire realm. They are villages, orchards, churches, farms, manors, and lanes that look familiar until something moves wrong.

Haunted forests, ghost roads, fallow villages, black manors, abandoned chapels, grave fields, and locked family houses give the domain its shape. Spirits, death marks, grave ash, forbidden formulae, and Bodachian study keep the dead involved in daily life.

Fear in the Fallows often begins in familiar rooms: a village inn, a family orchard, a chapel lane, a nursery, a cellar, a portrait hall, a road home after sunset.

Gloamspire rises near the center of the continent, a giant tower split from
crown to foundation by leaking blue-violet energy. Fog has swallowed its lower
districts. The inhabited tower, abandoned eastern ascent, drowned civic
archive, and dangerous buried engine reveal a city fighting to remember itself.
The open crown is the Fallows flight point.

The crown waysteward offers **The Ledger of Things That Persist**, a six-part
story for levels 200-250. It begins with a loose name-tag at Fallows Cross,
then compares testimony and physical records from every named region before
returning to the engine beneath Gloamspire. Use `engage <witness>` to hear an
account and `give <record> to registrar` when a contract calls for evidence.
The chain ends with Blackbriar, Vespercourt, and the thing the split tower was
built to contain.

## Areas

| Area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Gloamspire | Split tower-city, fog-swallowed lower wards, and dangerous buried works |
| Duskwood | Haunted forest where the day never feels complete |
| The Ghost Roads | Old lanes that move through memory as much as geography |
| Fallows Cross | Main village market, gallows square, chapel, and inn |
| Blackbriar Manor | Noble estate of locked doors, inheritance, mirrors, and old sin |
| The Hollow Chapels | Abandoned holy sites where bells and dead prayers remain active |
| Widowmere | Still lake where reflections answer before speakers finish |
| The Gravefields | Wide cemetery country with mausoleums, grave ash, and restless ground |
| Dawnward Redoubt | Mitran paladin camp holding a line of watchfires and mirrors against the fog |
| Moonwold Caverns | Werewolf dens where pack memory is kept in scent, scars, and stone |
| Vespercourt | Vampire court preserving bloodlines after every family name has begun to fade |

The Bodachian Fallows are about dread, investigation, and consequences. Players are pressed to ask what happened before they arrived, who benefits from silence, and which dead thing is still waiting to be paid.

The Fallows also give Mage a natural path into forbidden Bodach and Arcanarton study without making Necromancer a public guild.

Travelers can descend from the Aeolus sky to Gloamspire's crown, use
`tport fallows` at level 200, or unlock the smoke-dark wayshard in Gallows
Square. The eleven named regions are separated by a large fogbound wilderness
traversed by mistlings, not joined as one short road. The Shadow Cairn is
present in the Gravefields, but its Fallowsworn rite remains dormant.

Crooked Market in Gloamspire is a protected working shop. It carries
salt-thread travel gear, briarward boots, and Dawnward mirror salvage for the
opening progression band. Lord Blackbriar and Lady Vesper retain personal
contributor chests with level-250 regional equipment, while Namebearer remains
the continent's single scarce artifact.

Gloamspire's crown, the settled heart of Fallows Cross, and the defended
interior of Dawnward Redoubt are protected refuges. Their outer gates,
Dawnward's fogward breach, the continental roads, and hostile interiors are
not.
